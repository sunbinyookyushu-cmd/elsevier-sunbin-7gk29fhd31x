Rajya Sabha session: 229; Question No: 776; Type: UNSTARRED; Answer date: 13.08.2013
Ministry: CIVIL AVIATION
Title: Decrease in air passenger traffic
Member: Shri Piyush Goyal
Official file link: https://sansad.in/getFile/annex/229/Au776.html?source=pqars

QUESTION:
 (a) whether the passenger air traffic has decreased during the year 2012-13 over the<br/>preceding year 2011-12;<br/>(b) if so, the details thereof and the reasons therefor, particularly the impact of high<br/>cost of travel due to multiple taxes on aviation fuel and passenger/airports;<br/>(c) whether Government has formulated or proposes to formulate any plan to<br/>encourage people to undertake air travel;<br/>(d) if so, the details thereof; and<br/>(e) the details of the achievements made as a result thereof? 

ANSWER:
 MINISTER OF STATE IN THE MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION<br/>(SHRI K. C. VENUGOPAL)<br/><br/>(a) and (b) : The passenger traffic declined by 1.8 percent in 2012-13 compared to<br/>previous year i.e. 2011-12. The decline in the passenger traffic can mainly be<br/>attributed to the following reasons:<br/>(i) Global economic slow-down<br/>(ii)Sharp rise in the prices of ATF and higher operating costs<br/>(iii)poor financial health of domestic airline and<br/>(iv)suspension of flights by Kingfisher Airlines.<br/>(c) to (e): With a view to bring down the operating costs of the airlines, Ministry<br/>of Civil Aviation has taken up the matter relating to ATF prices and VAT on ATF<br/>with Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas and the State Governments<br/>respectively. Efforts of Ministry of Civil Aviation have resulted in reduction of<br/>VAT on ATF by some State Governments such as Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh and<br/>West Bengal. Further, with a view to make basic fare more affordable and to<br/>provide consumer an option of paying for the services which he wishes to avail,<br/>scheduled airlines have been allowed to unbundle services like preferential seating<br/>etc., to be availed by passengers on opt-in basis subject to compliance of<br/>requirements specified in Air Transport Circular No. 03/2013 issued by Directorate<br/>General of Civil Aviation (DGCA). Further, changes made in FDI policy in Civil<br/>Aviation Sector are also expected to intensify competition which could result in<br/>reduction of airfares.The overall impact of these steps cannot be assessed at this<br/>stage, however the Director General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) has noticed that<br/>domestic passenger traffic has shown some positive growth this year.<br/>*****
