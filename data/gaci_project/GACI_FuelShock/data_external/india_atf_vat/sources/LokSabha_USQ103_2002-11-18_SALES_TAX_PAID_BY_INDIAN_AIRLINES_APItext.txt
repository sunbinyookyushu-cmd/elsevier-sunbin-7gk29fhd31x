QUESTION:
 
 
 
 

(a) the amount of sales tax paid by Indian Airlines to 
 various States in 2001-2002; 
 
 

(b) whether sales tax rates on ATF vary in different States; 
 and 
 
 

(c) if so, the details thereof? 
 
 

ANSWER:
 
 
 MINISTER OF STATE IN THE MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION ( SHRI SHRIPAD YESSO NAIK ) 
 




 (a): The amount of Sales Tax paid by Indian Airlines and 
 Alliance Air to various States on Indian Airlines network, 
 during the year 2001-02, is Rs.17250.03 lacs. 
 

 
 (b) and (c): Yes, Sir. The Sales Tax on ATF varies from 4% 
 in the State of Andhra Pradesh to 39.10% in Kerala. A 
 statement showing the rates at which the Sales Tax on ATF is 
 levied by the States and Union Territories on Indian 
 Airlines network is annexed.
