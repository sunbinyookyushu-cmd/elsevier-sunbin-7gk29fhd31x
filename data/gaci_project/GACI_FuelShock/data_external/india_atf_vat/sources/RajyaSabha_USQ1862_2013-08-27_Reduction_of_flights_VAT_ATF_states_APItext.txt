Rajya Sabha session: 229; Question No: 1862; Type: UNSTARRED; Answer date: 27.08.2013
Ministry: CIVIL AVIATION
Title: Reduction of flights or frequency
Member: SHRIMATI T. RATNA BAI
Official file link: https://sansad.in/getFile/annex/229/Au1862.html?source=pqars

QUESTION:
 (a) whether it is a fact that due to heavy rise in operating costs airline operators<br/>have decided to reduce the number of flights and its frequency;<br/>(b) if so, the details thereof during the last three years including Air India flights;<br/>and<br/>(c) the steps being taken to correct the situation in this regard to reduce the<br/>economic losses of airline operators including Air India? 

ANSWER:
 MINISTER OF STATE IN THE MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION<br/>(SHRI K. C. VENUGOPAL)<br/>(a) and (b): No planned reduction has been observed in the number of domestic<br/>flights in the Summer schedule effective from 31st March 2013.<br/>(c): A Working Group was constituted under the Chairmanship of Secretary, Civil<br/>Aviation with Finance Secretary Secretary, Financial Services Secretary, M/o<br/>Petroleum & Natural Gas DGFT and Joint Secretary, Civil Aviation to discuss the<br/>factors causing stress in civil aviation and to suggest solutions to the same. Based<br/>on the recommendation of the Working Group, the Government has taken several<br/>measures to revive the aviation industry, reduce the economic losses and ensure<br/>long term viability of the sector:<br/>(i) Director General of Foreign Trade has allowed direct import of ATF by airlines<br/>on actual user basis.<br/>(ii) FDI by foreign airlines in the domestic scheduled and non scheduled carrier has<br/>been permitted upto 49 percent of their paid up capital.<br/>(iii) ECB upto $ USD 1 billion has been permitted for the airlines to meet their<br/>working capital requirement.<br/>(iv) The State Govt. has been persuaded to reduce the VAT on ATF. As a result,<br/>Govt. of Maharashtra, Govt. of Rajasthan, Gov. of Chhatisgarh, Govt. of<br/>Jharkhand and Govt. of West Bengal has reduced the VAT on ATF with certain conditions.<br/>*****
