Rajya Sabha session: 224; Question No: 2493; Type: UNSTARRED; Answer date: 15.12.2011
Ministry: CIVIL AVIATION
Title: Huge subsidy to Air India
Member: SHRI JAI PRAKASH NARAYAN SINGH
Official file link: https://sansad.in/getFile/annex/224/?source=pqars

QUESTION:
 (a) whether all airlines in India are operating 25 per cent below their operation cost;<br/><br/>(b) if so, the details in this regard;<br/>(c) whether Air India is being financially considered by Government in order to maintain operating cost;<br/><br/>(d) whether such a huge subsidy to Air India will hamper the growth of other airlines and make them bankrupt; and<br/><br/>(e) if so, the steps being taken to extend Government''s support to all airlines? 

ANSWER:
 THE MINISTER OF OVERSEAS INDIAN AFFAIRS & MINISTER OF CIVIL AVIAITON<br/>(SHRI VAYALAR RAVI)<br/><br/>(a) and (b):- No such assessement has been made by the Ministry. <br/><br/>(c):- Government has so far infused fresh equity of Rs.3200 crore in Air India and has constitued a Group of Ministers(GoM) to review/monitor the operational and financial performance of Air India. On the directions of GoM, Air India had prepared its Turn Around Plan(TAP) and Financial Restructuring Plan(FRP), which has been examined by a Group of Officers(GoO). The recommendations of GoO have been referred by the GoM for RBI’s views and regulatory forbearance on the FRP.<br/> <br/>(d):- No, Sir.<br/><br/>(e):- The single largest element contributing to the airline costs is the cost of Aviation Turbine Fuel (ATF) which accounts for 40% of the operation cost. One of the reasons of High ATF rates is high sales tax imposed by State Govts. The Government has undertaken measures to reduce the sales tax on ATF. These are as under :<br/><br/>(1) The State Governments have been persuaded to reduce the sales tax on ATF. Government of Chhatisgarh and with conditions cases Government of Rajasthan have reduced the sales tax on ATF to 4%. Government of Maharashtra has also reduced sales tax on ATF from 25% to 4% for flights originating from airports other than Pune and Mumbai. <br/>(2) The Oil companies have started announcing the ATF prices on a fortnightly basis instead of on monthly basis.
