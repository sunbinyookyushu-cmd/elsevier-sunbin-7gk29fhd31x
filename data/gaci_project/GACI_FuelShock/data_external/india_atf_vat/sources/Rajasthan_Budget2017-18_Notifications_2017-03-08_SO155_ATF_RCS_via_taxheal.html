Rajasthan Budget 2017-18 Notifications 08.03.2017

By CA Satbir Singh | March 8, 2017
0 Comment

Download : Following Notifications  in Rajasthan Budget 2017-18 dated 08.03.2017

Notification No-S.O.146 – Rajasthan Value Added Tax (Second Amendment) Rules, 2017.

Notification No S.O.147. –  the tax payable by the dealer who has exercised option for payment of tax under sub-section (2) of section 3 of the Act,

Notification No S.O.148.- Rate of tax payable for certian goods as provided in list

Notification No S.O.149.- amendment in Schedule I

Notification No  S.O.150.- amendment in Schedule II

Notification No S.O.151.- exempts from payment of tax payable by any dealer, as provided

Notification No S.O.152.-  exempts from payment of tax payable by any dealer who has opted for payment of lump sum in lieu of tax under notification number F.12(63)FD/ Tax/2005-39 dated 06.05.2006 (Composition Scheme for Saraffa Dealers, 2006),

Notification No S.O.153.-  effect, exempts from payment of tax payable by any dealer who has opted for payment of lump sum in lieu of tax under notification number F.12(63)FD/ Tax/2005-37 dated 06.05.2006 (Composition Scheme for Gems and Stones, 2006),

Notification No S.O.154.-  exempts from payment of tax payable by any dealer who has opted for payment of lump sum in lieu of tax under notification number F.12(28)FD/ Tax/2007/145 dated 09.03.2007 (Composition Scheme for registered dealers having retail outlets of Petroleum Companies),

Notification No S.O.155.-  exempts, with immediate effect, from the tax payable on the sale of Aviation Turbine

Fuel at RCS Airports located within the State

Notification No S.O.156.-  amendment in this department’s notification number F.12(161)FD/ Tax/09-1 dated 02.06.2014,

Notification No S.O.157  – exempts the sale to or purchase by Indian Railways, during the period from 18.08.2008 to 01.06.2014, of High Speed Diesel

Notification No S.O.158  with effect from 01.04.2015, makes the following amendments in this department’s notification number F.12(23)FD/Tax/2015-206 dated 09.03.2015,

Notification No S.O.159.- The Amnesty Scheme-2017.

Notification No S.O.160.- Rebate to the class of dealers as mentioned

Notification No S.O.161.- Central Sales Tax (Rajasthan) (Amendment) Rules, 2017.

Notification No S.O.162.-  amendment in this department’s notification number F.12 (99) FD / Tax / 07-66 dated 14.02.2008,

Notification No S.O.163.-  New Voluntary Amnesty Scheme for Entry Tax-2017.

Notification No S.O.164.-  with effect from 08.03.2016, exempts from tax payable under the Act on entry of all kinds of yarn into any local area of the State on the conditions:-

Notification No S.O.165.-  with effect from 01.08.2014, remits 100% entertainment tax leviable under section 4 of the said Act, on the amount charged for rendering the service of online booking of tickets

Notification No S.O.166 .- Amnesty Scheme for Entry Tax on Motor Vehicles -2017.

Notification No S.O.167  – exempts, with immediate effect, the electricity duty, the water conservation cess and the urban cess payable by the Jaipur Metro Rail Corporation on the energy consumed

S.O.168. –  stamp duty chargeable on the instrument of partition of property other than ancestral property shall be reduced and charged at the rate of 3 percent on the market value of the separated share or shares of the property.

S.O.169.- stamp duty chargeable on the instruments specified in clause (c) of Article 5 and sub-clause (ii) of clause (ee) of Article 44 of the Schedule to the said Act, shall be reduced and charged at the rate of 0.5 percent.

S.O.170.- to SO 198

FINANCE DEPARTMENT

(TAX DIVISION)

NOTIFICATION

Jaipur, March 08, 2017
