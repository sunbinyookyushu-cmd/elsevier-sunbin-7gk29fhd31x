s.4.4 Croatia (PDF p.68): "Croatia does not impose a ticket tax on air passengers. The country levies a VAT of 25% on domestic flights, but no excise duty on kerosene."
s.4.12 Greece (p.82): "Greece does not impose a ticket tax on air passengers. The country levies a VAT of 24% on domestic flights, but no excise duty on kerosene."  [note: the study does not treat the state TEAA/airport development fee as a ticket tax]
s.4.13 Hungary (p.84): "Hungary does not impose a ticket tax on air passengers."
s.4.19 Malta (p.94): "Malta levies no aviation taxes: neither a passenger ticket tax, nor a VAT on air tickets, nor an excise duty on kerosene."
s.4.22 Portugal (p.99): "Portugal does not impose a ticket tax on air passengers."
s.4.26 Spain (p.106): "Spain does not impose a ticket tax on air passengers. The country levies a VAT of 10% on domestic flights, except to/from the Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla. There is no excise duty on kerosene."
Caveat: snapshot as of 2018/2019 only.
