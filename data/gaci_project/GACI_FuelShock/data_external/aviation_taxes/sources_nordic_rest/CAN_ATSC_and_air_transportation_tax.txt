CANADA: Air Travellers Security Charge (ATSC) and Air Transportation Tax (ATT, Excise Tax Act Part II)
Accessed 2026-10-02

1) Air Travellers Security Charge Act, S.C. 2002, c. 9, s. 5 (current)
https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/A-10.5/FullText.html
 chargeable emplanement excludes an embarkation that "(i) is for the purpose of transferring from a particular flight to a connecting flight and (A) in the case of an air transportation service acquired in Canada, the particular flight included a chargeable emplanement by the individual, (B) the individual embarked on the particular flight outside Canada, or (C) the particular flight included an embarkation that, because of this subparagraph, is not a chargeable emplanement".
 s 7: applies to chargeable emplanements made after March 31, 2002 (service acquired in Canada) or after May 31, 2002 (acquired outside Canada).
 s 11(2)(a): charge payable at the time any consideration is first paid or becomes payable.
 s 12 history: 2002 c.9; 2003 c.15 s.44; 2005 c.19 s.2, c.30 s.20; 2006 c.4 s.33; 2007 c.35 s.196; 2010 c.12 s.96; 2023 c.26 s.127.

3) Excise Tax Act, R.S.C. 1985, c. E-15, Part II Air Transportation Tax
https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-15/page-2.html and page-3.html
 s 11(1)(a)(i): 4% of amount paid after December 31, 1997 for transportation beginning after February 28, 1998; 7% in any other case; plus $6 (or lesser prescribed amount).
 s 13(1): to outside taxation area: $30 (paid after Dec 31, 1997, travel beginning after Feb 28, 1998) or $55 in any other case.
 s 16.1 (1996, c.20, s.104; 1998, c.21, s.87): "No tax shall be imposed, levied or collected on any amount paid or payable for the transportation of a person by air that (a) in the case of tax imposed under subsection 10(1) or 12(1), begins after October 31, 1998".
 Air Transportation Tax Order, 1995 (SOR/95-206, as amended SOR/98-11): caps $30 (single segment self-ticketed $15) from 1 Mar 1998, previously $55 ($27.50); add-on $3 ($1.50) from 1 Mar 1998.
