2) Amending acts (as made PDFs on legislation.gov.au), Schedule 1 text:
 1998 (C2004A00326): Omit "$27", substitute "$30". Applies to departure on or after 1 January 1999.
 2001 (C2004A00847): Omit "$30", substitute "$38". Applies to departure on or after 1 July 2001, unless (a) the person departs using a ticket or equivalent authority; and (b) the ticket or authority was sold or issued before 1 July 2001.
 2008 (C2008A00058): Omit "$38", substitute "$47". Same rule, 1 July 2008.
 2012 (C2012A00094): Omit "$47", substitute "$55". Same rule, 1 July 2012.
 2016 No. 91 (C2016A00091): Omit "$55", substitute "$60". Commences 1 July 2017.
 2016 No. 2 (C2016A00093): adds "and does not increase from 1 July 2017 for 5 years thereafter."
 2024 (C2024A00006): Omit all the words after "Australia is", substitute "$70". Applies on or after 1 July 2024 unless ticket sold or issued before 1 July 2024.
 Norfolk Island 2016 (C2016A00032): para 4(3)(a) after "departs from" insert "Norfolk Island or".

3) Passenger Movement Charge Collection Act 1978 (compilation 2015-07-01), s 3 definition:
https://legislation.gov.au/C2004A01918/2015-07-01/2015-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html
 transit passenger: a person who arrives in Australia from a place outside Australia for the purpose only of a journey from that place to a place outside Australia and who departs (a) if arriving and departing by aircraft, without being immigration cleared within the meaning of s 172 Migration Act 1958 (or, if cleared for reasons beyond their control, as soon as reasonably practicable), or (b) in any other case, less than 48 hours after arrival.
 s 5 exemptions include persons under 12, crew, positioning crew, transit passengers, diplomats, certain defence personnel.

4) Air Passenger Ticket Levy (Imposition) Act 2001, No. 133, 2001 (assented 27 September 2001)
https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00916/asmade/2001-09-27/text/original/pdf
 s 6(2): "The rate of levy on an air passenger ticket is $10."
 Collection Act 2001 s 9(1): levy payable on an air passenger ticket that "(a) is purchased on or after 1 October 2001 and before the end of the final levy month; and (b) covers at least one relevant flight." Australian flight = "a flight that takes off from a place in Australia (whether the flight is to another place in Australia or to a place outside Australia)."
 ANAO Audit Report No. 21 2003-04 (SEESA): "The Levy ... applied to air passenger tickets purchased on or after 1 October 2001 until 30 June 2003."
