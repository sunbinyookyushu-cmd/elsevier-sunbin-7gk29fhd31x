## 1. Reference / citation format (TRA = Elsevier Harvard, author-year)
- Root cause: `\documentclass[review,3p,times]{elsarticle}` has no `authoryear` option, so elsarticle loads natbib in `numbers` mode and the PDF shows numbered citations even though `elsarticle-harv` is the bst. Fixed: `\documentclass[review,3p,times,authoryear]{elsarticle}`. In-text citations now render as (Author, year) / Author (year).
- DOIs now print as `https://doi.org/...` (TRA Guide for Authors sample), without the `doi:` / `URL:` prefixes; URLs in text font (`\urlstyle{same}`).
- Journal names abbreviated per ISO 4 / LTWA as in published TRA reference lists (e.g. J. Int. Econ., Q. J. Econ., Transp. Res. Part A Policy Pract.).
- `elsarticle-harv` sentence-cases titles, so proper nouns and acronyms were coming out lower-case (covid-19, china, u.s., eu, soviet ...). Brace-protected in 22 entries.
- Startz (2018) changed from a fake journal article ("SSRN Electronic Journal") to an SSRN working-paper entry.
