Reading: the headline -0.33 is not robust. It vanishes with country trends, with not-yet-treated controls,
and in the pre-2015 window; the country-level sign flips. Taxing countries (DE/AT/NL/Nordics) grew more
slowly than the never-taxed Southern/Eastern European leisure markets after 2010 for reasons unrelated to
the tax (LCC expansion, tourism boom), and region x year FE at the OAG-region level do not absorb that.
=> Identification must come from WITHIN-country variation: distance-band exposure (origin x destination
   country) with country x year FE, and the GACI-density IV. The binary cross-country DiD is descriptive.
