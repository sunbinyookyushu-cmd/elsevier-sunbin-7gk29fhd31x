# Air passenger ticket taxes: Nordic countries and other EU/EEA states

Companion note to `data/raw/taxes/taxes_NORDIC_OTHER.csv`. Compiled 2026-10-03 from web-search snippets of official and legislative sources (WebFetch unavailable; full-text documents could not be opened, so "confirmed" below means the value appears verbatim in an official-source snippet). Values that could not be confirmed are left empty in the CSV and flagged here. All rates are per departing passenger and apply to all classes unless stated otherwise.

---

## Sweden – flygskatt (1 Apr 2018 – 30 Jun 2025)

**Design.** Lag (2017:1200) om skatt på flygresor taxed each passenger departing from a Swedish airport on an aircraft approved for more than 10 passengers. Three bands defined by country annexes to the Act: Annex 1 (bilaga 1) = European countries; Annex 2 (bilaga 2) = non-European countries roughly within 6,000 km of Stockholm-Arlanda; all other final destinations. Exemptions (4 §): children under 2, crew on duty, transit (same aircraft) and transfer passengers, re-departures after weather/technical diversions. 8 § provided annual indexation to the general price level from 2019, fixed each autumn by government ordinance.

**Rates (SEK, Annex 1 / Annex 2 / other):**

| Period | B1 | B2 | B3 | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Apr 2018 – 31 Dec 2018 | 60 | 250 | 400 | SFS 2017:1200, 7 § |
| 2019 | 61 | 255 | 408 | Förordning (2018:1641) |
| 2020 | 62 | 260 | 416 | Förordning (2019:685) |
| 2021 | 63 | 262 | 418 | Förordning (2020:944) |
| 2022 | 64 | 265 | 424 | Förordning (2021:1082) |
| 2023 | 69 | 288 | 461 | Förordning (2022:1594) |
| 2024 | 76 | 315 | 504 | Förordning (2023:587) |
| 1 Jan – 30 Jun 2025 | 77 | 323 | 517 | Förordning (2024:924); Skatteverket rate table |

**Abolition.** Lag (2024:1138) om upphävande av lagen (2017:1200) om skatt på flygresor, issued 28 Nov 2024 (Prop. 2024/25:1), in force 1 July 2025. An April 2024 ministry memorandum ("Sänkt flygskatt") had proposed halving rates to 39/162/259 SEK from 2025; it was overtaken by the budget decision to abolish the tax. Revenue was roughly SEK 1.8 bn/yr.

**Confidence.** All eight rate-years and the abolition are confirmed against the SFS ordinances (high).

## Norway – flypassasjeravgift (1 Jun 2016 – present)

**Design.** An excise duty (særavgift) adopted by annual Stortinget decision (first decision 14 Dec 2015; detailed rules in Forskrift om særavgifter kap. 3-22), levied on commercial departures from Norwegian airports. Exemptions: children under 2; onward legs of transit/transfer journeys (only the first leg from a Norwegian airport is taxed). On domestic flights 12 % VAT is charged on top of the duty. Introduced 1 June 2016 (postponed from the planned 1 April 2016) at a flat rate. From 1 April 2019 it was split into a low rate (final destination in Norway/Europe, country list in the regulation) and a high rate (outside Europe).

## Denmark – passagerafgift (1991–2006) and passagerafgift på flyrejser (2025– )

**Old tax.** Lov om afgift af visse flyrejser replaced an earlier charter tax (charterafgift) in 1991. From 1 Sep 1991 the passenger tax was DKK 65 and applied only to international departures. The 1997 amendment (L 253, 1996–97) raised it to DKK 75 and extended it to domestic passengers from 1 Jan 1998. Revenue ≈ DKK 0.5 bn/yr. Lov nr. 1415 af 21. december 2005 phased it out: halved to DKK 37.50 from 1 Jan 2006 and abolished from 1 Jan 2007. Exemptions for the old tax were not verified from the sources consulted.

**New tax.** Political agreement "Grøn luftfart i Danmark" (Dec 2023) → L 184 (2023–24) → Lov nr. 620 af 11. juni 2024 om passagerafgift på flyrejser, in force 1 Jan 2025. Three bands by final destination: Annex 1 (Denmark + European countries), Annex 2 (medium-haul country list), other (long-haul). Exemptions: children under 2, transit/transfer passengers connecting within 24 h, crew on duty. § 3 legislates a rising schedule (DKK):

| Year | Europe | Medium | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 30 | 250 | 300 |
| 2026 | 30 | 260 | 320 |
| 2027 | 30 | 270 | 340 |
| 2028 | 40 | 280 | 350 |
| 2029 | 40 | 300 | 390 |
| 2030 | 50 | 310 | 410 |

From 2031 the amounts are indexed to the net price index; the 2030 amounts are stated in "2030-niveau". Average tax rises from ≈70 DKK (2025) to ≈100 DKK (2030). Note: the brief given to this task ("DKK 50/310/410 from 2025") corresponds to the **2030** end-point of the schedule, not the 2025 starting rates.

**Confidence.** High throughout (law text via lovguiden/tax.dk; Skatteministeriet agreement text; Folketingstidende for the old tax).

## Portugal – taxa de carbono sobre viagens aéreas (1 Jul 2021– )

Created by the 2021 State Budget (Lei n.º 75-B/2020, art. 390) and regulated by Portaria n.º 38/2021 de 16 de fevereiro: a flat **EUR 2** per passenger on commercial air (and maritime) travel departing from Portuguese territory, charged at ticket issuance, collected from 1 July 2021. Exemptions: children under 2; residents of the Azores and Madeira travelling between the mainland and their region or within the region. Portaria n.º 110/2023 extended the tax from 1 July 2023 to non-commercial/private flights in aircraft of up to 19 seats. No distance bands. Confidence: high.

## Belgium – taxe sur l'embarquement dans un aéronef / inschepingstaks (1 Apr 2022– )

Introduced by the Loi du 28 mars 2022 portant réduction de charges sur le travail (Moniteur belge 31 Mar 2022), payable by air carriers per passenger departing a Belgian airport from 1 April 2022: **EUR 10** if the final destination is < 500 km from Brussels Airport; **EUR 2** if ≥ 500 km and inside the EEA, UK or Switzerland; **EUR 4** otherwise. Exemptions: infants, active/repositioning crew, transit and transfer passengers, re-departures after forced landings. The Constitutional Court upheld the tax (arrêt 165/2023). Press reports in 2025 describe a planned restructuring under the new federal coalition (flat rate ≈ EUR 5); this was not verified as enacted and is not entered in the CSV. Confidence: high for the 2022 structure.

## Italy – addizionale comunale sui diritti d'imbarco (2004– )

Italy has no distance-banded ticket tax. Its passenger tax is a flat surcharge on the airport boarding fee, created by Legge 350/2003 art. 2 c. 11 (Finanziaria 2004) at EUR 1 to compensate airport municipalities for noise, then progressively raised and largely redirected to INPS (airline-sector solidarity fund / GIAS):

| From | National rate (EUR) | Change and legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Jan 2004 | 1.00 | L. 350/2003 art. 2 c. 11 |
| 2005 (≈ Apr) | 2.00 | +1.00, D.L. 7/2005 art. 6-quater c. 2, conv. L. 43/2005 (effective date not verified) |
| 1 Jan 2007 | 2.50 | +0.50, L. 296/2006 art. 1 c. 1328 |
| late Oct 2008 | 4.50 | art. 6-quater increment raised from 1 to 3 EUR by D.L. 134/2008 art. 2 c. 5-bis (conv. L. 166/2008) (effective date not verified) |
| 1 Jul 2013 | 6.50 | +2.00, L. 92/2012 art. 4 c. 75 (INPS Circ. 112/2013) |
| 1 Jan 2016 | 9.00 | +2.50 temporary increment (D.L. 145/2013 art. 13 c. 23; D.M. 357/2015) |
| 1 Sep 2016 | 6.50 | increment suspended, D.L. 113/2016 art. 13-ter |
| 1 Jan 2017 | 6.50 | increment abolished, L. 232/2016; still the national base rate |

Local add-ons: Rome Fiumicino/Ciampino carry an extra EUR 1 "addizionale commissariale" for Roma Capitale debt since D.L. 78/2010 art. 14 c. 14 (total EUR 7.50). Assaeroporti (Dec 2025) reports seven local levels between EUR 6.50 and 9.00 (e.g. Naples 9.00) following recent authorisations; these local variants are not itemised in the CSV beyond Rome. **IRESA** (imposta regionale sulle emissioni sonore degli aeromobili) is a regional aircraft-noise tax per movement, not per passenger, and is therefore out of scope. Exemption details (infants, transit) follow boarding-fee rules and were not verified. Confidence: high for 2004, 2007, 2013, 2016–2017 steps; medium for the exact effective dates of the 2005 and 2008 steps.

## Ireland – Air Travel Tax (30 Mar 2009 – 31 Mar 2014)

Finance (No. 2) Act 2008 s. 55: from 30 March 2009, **EUR 2** for departures to airports ≤ 300 km from Dublin Airport and **EUR 10** otherwise. After the European Commission objected to the distance differentiation (and opened a State-aid case later leading to recovery orders against Aer Lingus and Ryanair), Finance Act 2011 replaced it with a flat **EUR 3** from 1 March 2011. Budget 2014 (Oct 2013) announced abolition; the Air Travel Tax (Abolition) Order 2014 (S.I. No. 130 of 2014) ended the tax for departures on or after 1 April 2014. Confidence: high.

## Malta, Greece, Spain, Switzerland, Luxembourg

- **Malta.** A departure tax of Lm 10 (= EUR 23.29) per ticket was abolished with effect from 1 November 2008 after the European Commission opened infringement proceedings (decision to refer to the ECJ 27 June 2007; case frozen pending removal). The introduction date was not established (medium confidence on the amount; high on abolition).
- **Greece.** The Airport Development Fund charge (ΤΕΑΑ, "spatosimo", Law 2065/1992 art. 40 as amended by Law 2892/2001 and Law 4465/2017 art. 52) is an earmarked quasi-fiscal charge rather than a ticket tax (CE Delft 2019 does not list Greece). Pre-2017 it was EUR 12 for EU/Schengen destinations and EUR 22 (gtp 2017) or EUR 24 (gtp 2014) for non-Schengen destinations, on passengers over 5; a 31 Oct 2014 revision lowered some fees (amounts not confirmed). From the start of the Fraport Greece concession (11 Apr 2017) it became a flat EUR 12 at all airports on passengers over 2, and on 1 Nov 2024 it fell to EUR 3 as scheduled by Law 4465/2017 (Athens Airport raised its own PTF charge at the same time). Confidence medium/low for the pre-2017 structure, high for the 2024 cut.
- **Spain.** No national ticket tax; only AENA airport charges. A 2022 expert committee proposed a EUR 7.85 environmental ticket tax; not enacted.
- **Switzerland.** No ticket tax. The revised CO2 Act would have introduced a CHF 30–120 flight-ticket levy; rejected in the referendum of 13 June 2021 (51.6 % No).
- **Luxembourg.** No ticket tax (not among the seven European ticket-tax countries in CE Delft 2019).

## Other EU/EEA passenger ticket taxes (CE Delft 2019 and later)

CE Delft, *Taxes in the Field of Aviation and their impact* (for DG MOVE, June 2019) found ticket taxes in only seven EU/EEA countries: **Austria** (Flugabgabe, 2011: EUR 3.50 / 7.50 / 17.50 by distance band in 2018–19; reformed to EUR 12 flat in 2020–21), **France** (taxe de l'aviation civile EUR 4.52 / 8.14 and taxe de solidarité sur les billets d'avion EUR 1.13 economy intra-EEA up to EUR 45.07 business extra-EEA in 2019; both since merged into the TSBA), **Germany** (Luftverkehrsteuer, 2011: EUR 7.38 / 23.05 / 41.49 in 2019), **Italy** (surcharge above), **Norway**, **Sweden**, and the **United Kingdom** (Air Passenger Duty, by far the highest). Taxes introduced after the CE Delft study: **Netherlands** (vliegbelasting, 1 Jan 2021, EUR 7.845 flat, raised to EUR 26.43 in 2023 and indexed since), **Portugal** (2021), **Belgium** (2022) and **Denmark** (2025), all documented above. The AT/DE/FR/NL/UK figures here are context only (from T&E/Tax Foundation summaries of CE Delft) and are not entered in the CSV.
